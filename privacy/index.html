This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], registered under CNPJ [CNPJ NUMBER], with registered address at [COMPANY ADDRESS] ("Wanhap", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the Wanhap social media scheduling and publishing platform.

Wanhap is developed in Brazil and used internationally. We process personal data in accordance with Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) and, for users in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as applicable.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

When you sign up, we collect information such as your name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), and billing details processed by our payment provider. If you belong to an Account with multiple Workspaces, we also store your role and permissions within each Workspace.

1.2 Connected platform data

When you connect a social network or platform (Discord, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, Mastodon, Bluesky, TikTok, X, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Telegram), we receive profile information made available through that platform's authorization flow, such as your account name, handle, profile picture, and platform-assigned identifiers, along with an access token described in Section 2.

1.3 Content and media

We store the post content, captions, and media (images and video) you upload, draft, or schedule, including files stored in our S3-compatible object storage, and any content produced or modified through AI-assisted features.

1.4 Usage data

We collect information about how you use Wanhap, such as log data, device and browser information, IP address, pages visited, and actions taken in the product, for security, analytics, and product improvement purposes.

2. OAuth Tokens and Connected Accounts

To publish on your behalf, Wanhap uses the OAuth authorization flow provided by each Connected Platform. This grants us an access token (and, where applicable, a refresh token) rather than your platform password. These tokens are encrypted at rest and are used only to perform the actions you request — such as publishing a scheduled post, fetching basic profile data, or refreshing the connection — and only within the scopes you authorized during the connection process.

You can disconnect a Connected Platform at any time from your Workspace settings. Disconnecting revokes Wanhap's ability to use the corresponding token and triggers deletion of the stored token, subject to normal processing time.

3. AI-Assisted Features

When you use an AI-assisted feature (such as post drafting, content review, text humanization, or image generation and regeneration), the relevant text or media is sent to a third-party AI provider for processing in order to produce the output you requested. We select providers that offer contractual data protection commitments, but we do not control how those providers technically process data on their infrastructure. Do not use AI-assisted features on content you are not comfortable sharing with a third-party AI provider for processing.

4. Cookies and Session Data

We use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to maintain your session, as well as cookies for basic product analytics and security (such as detecting suspicious login activity). We do not use cookies to serve third-party advertising.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal data. We share information with the following categories of recipients, only as necessary for them to provide their service to us:

  • Payment processing: Stripe, to process subscription billing and payments.
  • AI providers: third-party AI providers, to deliver AI-assisted features described in Section 3.
  • Cloud and storage infrastructure: our S3-compatible object storage provider and hosting infrastructure, to store and serve your account data, content, and media.
  • Transactional email providers: to deliver account, billing, and product notifications.
  • Connected Platforms: the specific social network you choose to publish to, limited to the content and actions you schedule.
  • Legal and safety: where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Wanhap or others.

These recipients are only permitted to process personal data for the purposes described above and are bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account data, content, and connected-platform data for as long as your Account is active. If your Account is suspended for a violation of our Terms of Use, or you request deletion, Wanhap applies a 30-day grace period before permanent deletion, during which the request or suspension can still be reversed. Once the grace period ends, your data is permanently deleted from our production systems, except where we are required to retain certain records (such as billing records) for longer to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the LGPD, GDPR, or other applicable data protection law, including the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal data, and access it;
  • Correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to Section 6 above;
  • Request portability of your data to another provider, where technically feasible;
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Brazil, the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados — ANPD).

You can exercise most of these rights directly from your account settings, or by contacting us at [DATA PROTECTION CONTACT EMAIL].

8. Children's Privacy

Wanhap is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it.

9. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including encryption of OAuth tokens and sensitive credentials at rest, access controls limiting who within Wanhap can access production data, and encrypted transmission of data between your browser and our systems. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Because Wanhap serves users internationally and relies on service providers that may process data outside your country of residence, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including Brazil. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognized under applicable law (such as LGPD-compliant transfer mechanisms or GDPR standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the service or by email before the changes take effect.

12. Contact
